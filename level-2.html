Focus Areas

Create space with control.

  • Change of pace and change of direction
  • Protected dribbles against pressure
  • Jab steps, rip-throughs, and first-step attacks
  • Finishing from different angles
  • Midrange balance and shot preparation

Weekly Checklist

Build repeatable counters.

  • Complete the Level 2 PDF workout twice
  • Make 30 finishes with each hand
  • Practice 3 separation moves at game pace
  • Track your best weak-hand control round

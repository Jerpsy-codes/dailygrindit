Focus Areas

Own the details first.

  • Athletic stance and ready position
  • Right-hand and left-hand ball control
  • Form shooting and follow-through
  • Layup footwork on both sides
  • Chest pass, bounce pass, and target passing

Weekly Checklist

Complete before moving up.

  • Finish the full PDF workout at least twice
  • Make 50 form shots with correct balance
  • Complete 3 ball-control rounds without stopping
  • Record one short drill video for feedback

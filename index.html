DAILY GRIND INDIVIDUAL TRAINING (DGIT) Rules and Regulations

  1. Payment-First Policy

    The training fee must be paid in full before the training begins or no later than the first day of training.

  2. Venue Availability and Rescheduling

    Solo or private training sessions are treated as walk-in sessions at the venue. If the venue receives another confirmed booking or rental during the scheduled training time, the session may need to be rescheduled.

  3. Cancellation Policy

    Training-session cancellations must be made at least five hours before the scheduled session. Failure to cancel within the required time will result in the deduction of one session from the athlete’s package.

  4. Late Arrival Policy

    Late athletes may still attend the session, but the training will end at the originally scheduled time. Athletes are advised to arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to maximize their training time.

  5. Package Validity

    All purchased training packages must be used within one month from the starting date. Any unused sessions remaining after one month will be considered expired and will no longer be available.

By submitting this booking request, you confirm that you understand the payment, cancellation, late-arrival, venue-rescheduling, and package-validity policies.

Submitting this form does not automatically confirm your training schedule. Coach Tommy will review your booking request and proof of payment. He will contact you through Facebook or Gmail to arrange your schedule.

You may also contact Coach Tommy after submitting your request, but please avoid submitting the same booking multiple times.

Head Coach

Coach Tommy Spencer Ugsimar

Coach Tommy leads DGIT with a premium training standard: discipline first, details always, and player habits that translate from workouts into real games.

Coach Tommy Spencer founded the Daily Grind training system in 2020. Since then, he has worked with Cebu school varsity players, UAAP and NCAA athletes, and professional basketball players.

Through DailyGrindIT, he has helped more than 2,000 athletes improve their overall basketball skills, confidence, and understanding of the game.

  • Fundamentals, footwork, finishing, and shooting mechanics
  • Basketball IQ, confidence, and game-ready decision making
  • Structured feedback for athletes and families
